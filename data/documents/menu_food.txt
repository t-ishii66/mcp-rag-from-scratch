The Wandering Kettle - Food Menu

ALL DAY PLATES

Soft scrambled eggs on sourdough - $6.20
Mushroom toast with thyme - $6.60
Soup of the day with bread - $5.40
Rice porridge with pickled plum - $5.00

The soup changes daily and is written on the small slate by the register. On
Mondays it is almost always root vegetable. The kitchen stops taking plate
orders thirty minutes before closing, so arrive earlier if you are hungry.

SANDWICHES

Ham and comte on baguette - $5.80
Roasted vegetable and hummus - $5.60
Egg salad on milk bread - $4.90
Tuna and cucumber - $5.40

Sandwiches are made to order and take about eight minutes. If you are in a
hurry, tell the person at the register and they will suggest something from
the pastry case instead.

PASTRY CASE

Croissant - $2.50
Almond croissant - $3.20
Cinnamon roll - $3.10
Lemon poppy seed cake - $2.90
Banana bread - $2.80

Pastries are delivered at seven in the morning from a bakery two streets away.
Whatever is left after four in the afternoon is sold at half price. Almond
croissants usually disappear before noon on weekends.

The banana bread is the only item we bake ourselves, using fruit that has gone
too soft to serve any other way.
