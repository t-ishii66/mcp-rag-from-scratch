The Wandering Kettle - Ingredients and Dietary Notes

PLEASE TELL US

Tell the person at the register before you order. The kitchen is small and we
would rather adjust a dish than apologise for one.

WITHOUT MILK

Oat milk and soy milk are both available at no extra charge, and every
espresso drink can be made with either. The thick drinking chocolate is made
with dairy and cannot be changed, because the recipe depends on it.

The banana bread and the lemon poppy seed cake both contain butter. The
roasted vegetable sandwich and the rice porridge contain no dairy at all.

WITHOUT WHEAT

We have no gluten free bread. The rice porridge and the soup of the day are
usually safe, but the soup sometimes uses a stock that contains barley, so ask
on the day. Pastries all contain wheat.

NUTS

Almond croissants are made with almond paste and are handled on the same
surface as the other pastries. If a nut allergy is severe, we cannot promise
that any item from the pastry case is free of traces.

We do not use peanuts anywhere in the kitchen.

WITHOUT ANIMAL PRODUCTS

Fully plant based at the moment: the roasted vegetable and hummus sandwich,
the mushroom toast if we leave off the butter, the rice porridge, and the
yuzu soda. Most days the soup is as well, but not always.

EGGS

Egg salad and the scrambled eggs are the obvious ones. The croissant and the
cinnamon roll are brushed with egg wash before baking.

A NOTE ON THE KITCHEN

One preparation surface, one oven, one small sink. Everything is handled near
everything else, so we cannot describe any item as free from traces of the
things listed above.
