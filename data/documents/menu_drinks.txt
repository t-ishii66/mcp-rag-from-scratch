The Wandering Kettle - Drink Menu

ESPRESSO BAR

Espresso - $2.80
Americano - $3.20
Flat White - $4.00
Cafe Latte - $4.20
Mocha - $4.60

Every espresso drink is pulled from our house blend, "Harbour Fog". We roast
it downstairs every Tuesday morning, so Wednesday is usually the best day to
taste it. Espresso drinks arrive steaming in a heavy ceramic cup that keeps
its temperature for a good twenty minutes. Ask for it over ice and we will
pour the same shot over a tall glass of cubes instead.

BREWED COFFEE

Pour-over, single origin, rotates weekly - $4.40
Cold brew, steeped eighteen hours - $4.00
Refill of the day's brewed coffee - $1.00

The single origin changes every Monday. In winter we lean toward Guatemalan
beans with a heavier body; in summer we pour something brighter from Ethiopia.
Ask the barista what is open right now and they will offer you a small taste
before you commit to a cup.

TEA AND OTHER THINGS

Assam with steamed milk - $3.60
Genmaicha - $3.40
Chamomile with honey - $3.50
Thick drinking chocolate - $4.80
Yuzu soda - $3.90

The drinking chocolate is our most requested item once the temperature drops
below ten degrees. It is closer to melted chocolate than to cocoa, and we
serve it in a small cup because most people cannot finish a large one.

Chamomile and genmaicha are both caffeine free, which makes them the usual
choice for our late evening regulars.
