The Wandering Kettle - How This Place Started

THE OWNER

Mira Halloran opened the room above the bicycle shop in the spring of 2016.
She had spent eleven years as a structural engineer and left after a project
she had worked on for three years was cancelled in a single afternoon. She
wanted, in her own words, work where the result of the day was visible by the
end of the day.

She had never worked in a cafe before. The first six months were, by her own
account, mostly mistakes.

THE NAME

The kettle came from her grandmother's house in Cork and had already moved
between four addresses before it arrived here. It does not work and has never
been used to make anything. It sits on the shelf above the till, dented on one
side, and it gave the place its name.

THE ROASTING

The small drum roaster in the basement was bought secondhand in 2018 from a
company that was closing. Before that we bought beans already roasted from a
supplier in Yokohama.

Roasting our own changed the economics more than it changed the flavour. It
also meant closing one day a week, which nobody expected to be the hardest
part of the decision.

The house blend is named after the fog that comes up from the river in
November.

THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

The bicycle shop downstairs has been there since 1978 and its owner, Mr Endo,
holds the spare key. The bakery two streets away opened the year after we did
and has supplied our pastries from the beginning.

We are not trying to grow. There has been one location for nine years and
there are no plans for a second.
