The Wandering Kettle - Hours and Directions

WHEN WE ARE OPEN

Monday: 8:00 to 18:00
Tuesday: 8:00 to 18:00
Wednesday: closed
Thursday: 8:00 to 20:00
Friday: 8:00 to 22:00
Saturday: 9:00 to 22:00
Sunday: 9:00 to 17:00

We are closed every Wednesday because that is when the roasting happens and
the whole ground floor smells of smoke. We also close for the first week of
January and for three days in mid August.

The last order is taken fifteen minutes before we lock the door.

WHERE WE ARE

3-14-2 Kitasawa, second floor, above the bicycle repair shop. There is no
sign at street level, only a green door with a small kettle painted on it.
Take the stairs on the left of the shop entrance.

GETTING HERE

The nearest station is Kitasawa on the Inokashira line, about six minutes on
foot. Leave by the south exit, walk toward the river, and turn right at the
second traffic light.

From Shibuya it is roughly fifteen minutes by train and then the walk above.

PARKING AND BICYCLES

We have no car park. The coin lot behind the post office charges $2.00 per
hour and is the closest option.

Bicycles can be left in the rack beside the green door. There is room for
about eight. On weekend afternoons it fills up, and the police do ticket
bicycles left on the pavement itself.
