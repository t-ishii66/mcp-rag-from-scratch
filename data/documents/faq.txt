
The Wandering Kettle - Questions We Get Asked

CAN I STAY AND WORK?

Yes, on most days. We ask for one order every ninety minutes, and we ask you
to move to a smaller table if the room fills up. On Friday and Saturday after
six in the evening we put the laptops away and the room becomes a place for
conversation instead.

IS THERE WIRELESS INTERNET?

Yes. The network is called kettle-guest and the password is printed on the
back of the paper coaster under your cup. It changes every month.

POWER OUTLETS

There are six sockets along the window bench and two more beside the shelf of
secondhand books. The four small tables in the middle of the room have none,
which is deliberate.

DO YOU TAKE RESERVATIONS?

Not for one or two people. For groups of five or more, send a message the day
before and we will hold the long table by the window.

PAYMENT

Cash, all the usual contactless cards, and the two common QR payment apps.
We do not accept cheques or foreign currency. There is no minimum spend for
card payment and we do not add a service charge.

ANIMALS

Dogs are welcome on the small terrace at the top of the stairs, but not
inside the room itself. There is a water bowl by the terrace door. Assistance
animals are welcome anywhere.

CHILDREN

Very welcome. We have two high chairs and a box of wooden blocks under the
book shelf. We do not have a changing table.

TAKEAWAY

Everything on the drink menu can go in a paper cup. Bring your own container
and we take 50 cents off. We stopped offering plastic lids in 2023.

LOST PROPERTY

Anything left behind is kept in a crate behind the counter for one month.
Umbrellas, mostly.
