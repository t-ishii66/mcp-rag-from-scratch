The Wandering Kettle - Notes for Staff

OPENING

Arrive forty minutes before the door opens. Switch on the espresso machine
first, because it needs half an hour to come up to pressure. While it heats:
grind a first dose and throw it away, wipe down the window bench, fill the
water jug, put the slate outside, and count the float in the register. The
float is $300 in mixed coins and notes.

Check the grinder setting with a test shot. Aim for thirty grams out of
eighteen grams in, in about twenty eight seconds. Humidity moves this around
more than anything else, so expect to adjust on rainy mornings.

CLOSING

Last order is fifteen minutes before the posted closing time. After the final
customer leaves: backflush the machine, empty and rinse the drip tray, run the
grinder empty, wipe the steam wands, take the cash to the safe upstairs, and
put the milk that will not survive until tomorrow into the staff fridge.

Turn the water heater off at the wall. It has been left on twice and it is
expensive.

SHIFTS

Two people on the floor at all times, three on Friday and Saturday evenings.
Swap shifts freely, but write the swap on the paper calendar in the back room
so that nobody has to guess.

Breaks are thirty minutes for a shift over five hours, taken upstairs and not
behind the counter.

WHAT TO WEAR

Whatever you like, with an apron over it. Closed shoes, because we drop things.
Long hair tied back when you are in the kitchen.

IF SOMETHING BREAKS

The espresso machine has a service contract; the number is taped inside the
cupboard door under the sink. Do not attempt to open the boiler. For anything
else, tell the owner and she will decide whether it is worth repairing.
